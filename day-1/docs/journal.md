# Day 1 Journal

> Written after the day is done. The campfire reflection.
> Be honest. Nobody's grading this.

---

**What did I study today?**

*(One to three sentences. The topic, the resource, how long.)*

---

**What finally clicked?**

*(The one thing that went from "huh?" to "OH." Be specific — what was the
moment? What made it click? A diagram? A sentence? Writing it in code?)*

---

**What still confuses me?**

*(Don't pretend you understood everything. Future-Duke — and future-reader —
need to know what's still foggy. This is the most honest section.)*

---

**What did I gain today?**

*(Not just what you learned — what changed? How do you think differently
now than you did this morning? Even a tiny shift counts.)*

---

**Vibe check**

*(How did today feel? Excited? Frustrated? Lost? Surprisingly good?
One or two sentences. Real talk.)*
